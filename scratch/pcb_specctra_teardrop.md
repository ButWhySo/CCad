## Specctra & Teardrop
- **specctra_import_export**: (`specctra.h/cpp`) DSN (Specctra) format exporter and importer, typically used for external autorouters like Freerouting.
- **teardrop**: (`teardrop.h/cpp`) Procedural geometry generation for teardrops (fillets between tracks and pads/vias to reduce mechanical stress and acid traps).
